The button didn't open your calendar? Subscribe with this address

  • Outlook: Add calendar, then Subscribe from web.
  • Apple Calendar on Mac: File, then New Calendar Subscription.
  • iPhone / iPad: Settings, Calendar, Accounts, Add Account, Other, Add Subscribed Calendar.
  • Other apps: look for "Subscribe to calendar" or "Add from URL".

Menu names vary a little between versions. The calendar then updates itself, and new seminars can take a few hours to appear.
