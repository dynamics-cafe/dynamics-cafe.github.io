1Identity of the organisers and scope of this notice

Dynamics Cafe is an independent, voluntary and non-commercial online seminar series on dynamical systems. This notice explains how personal data are processed when you propose or give a talk, subscribe to seminar announcements, contact the organisers, attend an online seminar or interact with the series through its website and public communication channels.

The organisers are Ilaria Cunico, based in Trieste, Italy; Matteo Gallo, based in Trieste, Italy; Matteo Allione, based in Trieste, Italy; Aran Dabbaghchi, based in Exeter, United Kingdom; and Valentina Barletta, based in Basel, Switzerland. They act in their personal capacity and jointly determine the purposes and essential means of the processing described in this notice. They therefore act as joint controllers within the meaning of Article 26 of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR) and, where applicable, the UK GDPR. Their processing is also subject to the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) to the extent that it applies.

The organisers' affiliations with OGS, the University of Trieste, SISSA, the University of Exeter and the University of Basel identify their academic backgrounds only. These institutions do not organise the series and are not identified as controllers for the processing described here.

The common contact address is dynamics.cafe.seminars@gmail.com. You may write in English or Italian. This address may be used for any question, request or complaint concerning your personal data. You may also exercise your rights against any individual organiser.

References below to GDPR legal bases describe the basis relied upon under the EU GDPR and, where applicable, the UK GDPR. They do not imply that the Swiss FADP requires an identical system of legal bases. The organisers apply the relevant Swiss requirements where applicable.

2Speakers and public announcements

If you agree to give a talk, the organisers process your name, affiliation, email address, correspondence, talk title, abstract and keywords, together with any photograph you choose to provide. These data are obtained from you or from a person submitting the material on your behalf. If material is supplied by someone else, the organisers provide you with this notice and seek your authorisation before publication.

Correspondence and the practical arrangements for the seminar are processed on the basis of the organisers' legitimate interest in organising and delivering the series, under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. This includes arranging the date, discussing the proposed content and sending joining instructions.

Publication of your name, affiliation, title, abstract and keywords is based on your prior consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR. Before asking for that consent, the organisers identify the intended channels and explain any continued publication in the archive. The channels are the website at https://dynamics-cafe.github.io, its public GitHub repository, the Dynamics Cafe LinkedIn page at https://www.linkedin.com/company/dynamics-cafe, the public Google Calendar and seminar announcement emails. Only the information relevant to each channel is published. Your email address and private correspondence are not included in public announcements.

Your photograph is optional. Its publication requires a separate affirmative consent identifying the channels concerned, which may include the website, LinkedIn and announcement emails. Consent to publish the text of a talk does not authorise publication of a photograph. You may give a talk without consenting to the use of your photograph. Silence or failure to object is not treated as consent.

Where you have agreed to archival publication, the website and the associated public repository retain the authorised talk information, and any separately authorised photograph, as a permanent archive of the series. This material remains available indefinitely, until you withdraw consent or ask for its removal. LinkedIn announcements and public calendar entries may also remain available as records of past events.

Private correspondence concerning an accepted talk is retained for 12 months after the seminar or the end of the related correspondence, whichever is later. Original photograph files held in the mailbox are deleted within 30 days after the talk; any authorised public version remains subject to the archival rules above.

Evidence of consent is retained while the corresponding publication continues and for 12 months afterwards, on the basis of the organisers' legitimate interest in demonstrating compliance and handling any related dispute. A longer period applies only where necessary to meet a specific legal obligation or address an actual dispute.

3Proposals and general correspondence

When you propose a talk, the organisers process your name, contact details, affiliation and proposal to evaluate its suitability and communicate their decision. The legal basis is their legitimate interest in selecting and arranging seminars under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Proposals that are declined, withdrawn or otherwise closed without a seminar taking place are deleted, with the related correspondence, within 6 months after closure. Accepted proposals are handled under section 2.

When you contact the common email address, the organisers process your name, email address and message to answer your enquiry and manage any follow-up. This processing is based on their legitimate interest in responding to communications concerning the series. General correspondence is deleted within 12 months after the conversation ends. Correspondence concerning a talk, a proposal or a privacy request follows the specific retention provisions in this notice.

Messages sent through LinkedIn or other platforms may also remain on those platforms under their own retention rules. The organisers can manage only the content and records within their control.

4Mailing list and seminar announcements

If you subscribe to the mailing list, the organisers process your email address and the records needed to demonstrate your subscription and confirmation, including dates and times and any technical confirmation information recorded by the mailing service. These data are used only to send Dynamics Cafe seminar announcements and manage your subscription.

Subscription is based on consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR. The mailing list uses a double opt-in process: you submit your address and then confirm it through a link sent by email. Supplying an email address in other correspondence or attending a seminar does not automatically subscribe you to the mailing list.

EmailOctopus (Three Hearts Digital Ltd, United Kingdom) operates the mailing list on behalf of the organisers under the data processing schedule of its terms. The service is configured not to track email opens or clicks. Technical information necessary to confirm subscriptions, deliver messages and process unsubscribes may nevertheless be processed by the service.

You may unsubscribe using the link in an announcement or by emailing the common contact address. Announcement emails stop when the unsubscribe request is implemented. Unsubscribed contact profiles are deleted within 3 months. Limited records of consent and withdrawal may be retained for up to 12 months after unsubscribing to demonstrate compliance. If an exclusion record is needed to prevent accidental resubscription, it is limited to the minimum identifier required and is not used to send announcements. Unconfirmed subscriptions are deleted within 3 months.

5Online seminars

Seminars take place through Google Meet. During a seminar, the organisers and other participants may see your chosen display name and anything you voluntarily share through your camera, microphone or chat. The organisers process these data only to facilitate the live seminar, on the basis of their legitimate interest in delivering the series under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

The organisers do not record seminars, export or retain participant lists, or save chat messages. They may record the total attendance as an aggregate number that does not identify participants. You may keep your camera off and use a display name that does not reveal your full name, subject to the platform's access requirements. Please avoid sharing sensitive or unnecessary personal information during the session.

Google may process account, connection, security and service-use information under its applicable terms and privacy notice. The organisers' decision not to retain seminar data does not determine Google's own retention practices.

6Website, public calendar and LinkedIn

The website is hosted through GitHub Pages. The organisers do not use website analytics, advertising trackers or tracking cookies and do not embed third-party calendars or other third-party content. The hosting provider may process IP addresses and other technical information for operating and securing its service under the GitHub Privacy Statement. The organisers do not have access to the hosting provider's visitor logs.

The Google Calendar is public and contains the authorised information about scheduled seminars. Subscribing to it through your own calendar application does not provide the organisers with a subscriber list. If you interact with an event in another way, including responding to an invitation or sending a message, the information visible to the organisers is handled for the relevant organisational purpose. Personal contact addresses are not intentionally published in the calendar.

LinkedIn provides aggregated Page Insights about the audience and reach of the Dynamics Cafe page. The organisers use these statistics to understand how seminar announcements reach the research community, relying on their legitimate interest under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. For the personal-data processing underlying Page Insights, the organisers and LinkedIn Ireland Unlimited Company act as joint controllers under LinkedIn's Page Insights Joint Controller Addendum. LinkedIn undertakes the obligations allocated to it under that addendum. The organisers receive aggregate statistics rather than the underlying individual-level analytics data; names or profiles may separately be visible when people follow, comment on or otherwise interact with the page.

For LinkedIn's other processing, including account management, recommendations and advertising, LinkedIn's privacy policy applies. The same principle applies when you follow a link to another external service: its own terms and privacy notice govern the processing it carries out. The absence of tracking on the Dynamics Cafe website does not mean that external platforms use no cookies or tracking technologies.

7Recipients and service providers

Personal data are accessible to the five organisers to the extent needed to perform their tasks. Authorised public information is accessible to the public, including users outside Europe, and may be copied, indexed or shared by third parties.

The organisers use Google services for the common Gmail account, Google Meet and Google Calendar; GitHub for website hosting and the public repository; LinkedIn for the public page; and EmailOctopus for mailing-list management when that service is used. EmailOctopus acts as a processor for mailing-list data under the data processing schedule of its terms. Other providers process data according to the service and applicable contractual terms, including as independent controllers for their own account, security and service-management purposes. The Page Insights arrangement with LinkedIn is described in section 6. Use of a platform does not transfer the organisers' responsibility for their own processing to that platform.

Google's responsible entity for consumer services depends on the user's location and applicable service notice, including Google Ireland Limited for users in the EEA and Switzerland and Google LLC for users in the United Kingdom. GitHub's relevant provider is GitHub, Inc.; the LinkedIn entity identified for Page Insights is LinkedIn Ireland Unlimited Company. EmailOctopus's contracting entity and processing arrangements are governed by the terms applicable to the organisers' account.

Data may also be disclosed to competent authorities where legally required, or to advisers where necessary to address a specific legal matter, subject to appropriate confidentiality safeguards. The organisers do not sell personal data.

8International disclosures and safeguards

The organisers access and share data from Italy, the United Kingdom and Switzerland. Disclosures from the EEA to the United Kingdom and Switzerland rely on the European Commission's applicable adequacy decisions. Corresponding disclosures from the United Kingdom and Switzerland are made under the adequacy arrangements applicable in those jurisdictions.

Google and GitHub may process data in the United States. For transfers covered by a provider's valid certification, the applicable EU-US, UK Extension or Swiss-US Data Privacy Framework may provide the transfer mechanism. Where certification does not cover the recipient or processing concerned, an appropriate alternative safeguard is required, such as standard contractual clauses together with the applicable UK or Swiss adaptations and any necessary supplementary measures. A certification is relied upon only within its scope.

EmailOctopus is established in the United Kingdom, which benefits from an EU adequacy decision, and stores mailing-list data in the EEA (currently Ireland), with processing locations and subprocessors identified in its data processing schedule. European hosting does not by itself exclude every form of international access or onward transfer. The safeguards applicable to those operations are governed by the relevant processing terms.

You may request information about the safeguards applicable to the organisers' disclosures and, where relevant, a copy or reference to them by emailing the common contact address. Publicly authorised speaker information is accessible worldwide. That accessibility is disclosed as part of the publication requested; consent to public publication does not replace any transfer safeguard otherwise required.

9Your choices and rights

No law requires you to subscribe, propose a talk, provide a photograph or participate in a seminar. An email address is necessary to receive announcements or a reply. A proposal needs enough information for the organisers to assess it. Without consent to publish the relevant talk information, the organisers cannot announce you publicly. Refusing or withdrawing consent to a photograph does not prevent participation as a speaker.

Under the EU GDPR and, where applicable, the UK GDPR, you may request access to your personal data, correction of inaccurate data, erasure or restriction of processing, subject to the conditions and exceptions provided by law. Where the requirements of Article 20 GDPR are met, you may receive data you provided and that are processed by automated means on the basis of consent in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format, and request their transmission to another controller where technically feasible. You may object, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to processing based on legitimate interests. Rights under the Swiss FADP, including access, correction and data delivery or transfer where applicable, remain available.

You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting the organisers. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal. Publication based on the withdrawn consent will cease, and the organisers will remove the relevant information from the channels within their control without undue delay. This includes relevant website content, public repository content, calendar entries and LinkedIn publications. Where historical repository versions or platform caches retain the material, the organisers will take reasonable and proportionate steps, including seeking provider assistance where necessary, and explain any remaining limitations.

The organisers cannot recall emails already delivered or guarantee removal of independent copies, reshares, repository forks, search-engine caches or third-party web archives. Where the legal conditions apply, they will take reasonable steps to inform other controllers processing the published data of an erasure request. They will explain the action taken rather than promise outcomes beyond their control.

Requests should be sent to dynamics.cafe.seminars@gmail.com. The organisers respond without undue delay and, for GDPR requests, normally within one month of receipt. Where the applicable law permits an extension for complexity or number of requests, they notify you within that first month and explain the reasons. Any information needed to verify your identity is limited to what is necessary and proportionate. Requests are normally free of charge, subject to the statutory exceptions for manifestly unfounded or excessive requests.

Records of privacy requests and complaints are retained for 12 months after closure, on the basis of the organisers' legitimate interest in documenting their response and compliance, unless a specific legal obligation or an ongoing dispute requires longer retention.

10Complaints and supervisory authorities

You may complain to the organisers through the common contact address. They acknowledge complaints within 30 days, investigate them, keep the complainant appropriately informed and communicate the outcome without undue delay. This does not restrict your right to contact a supervisory authority or seek a judicial remedy.

For matters subject to the EU GDPR, you may complain to a competent supervisory authority, particularly in the Member State of your habitual residence, place of work or the alleged infringement. The Italian authority is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, www.garanteprivacy.it. For UK matters, you may contact the Information Commissioner's Office, ico.org.uk. For Swiss matters, you may contact the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner, www.edoeb.admin.ch, subject to the remedies available under Swiss law.

11Joint responsibilities, security and updates

The organisers have established a common contact point and an internal arrangement for their joint processing. They jointly decide the purposes and essential means, ensure that the information given to individuals remains accurate, and coordinate subscription management, publication, retention, security and responses to requests. Requests received by any organiser are brought to the attention of the group and handled through the common contact point. The organiser handling a matter keeps the others informed. Access is removed when an organiser leaves. The internal allocation of tasks does not limit your right to exercise GDPR rights against any of the joint controllers.

Access to the common accounts is restricted to the organisers and protected by strong credentials and two-factor authentication. Private correspondence, consent records and unpublished proposals are kept out of the public repository. Photographs enter public channels only where the relevant publication has been authorised. Retention periods are monitored and applied to the records controlled by the organisers.

The organisers do not carry out profiling or make decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects based solely on automated processing. The series is intended for an academic audience and does not deliberately solicit personal data from children.

This notice will be updated when the organisation of the series or its processing changes. Material changes affecting data already collected will be brought to the attention of the people concerned before the new processing begins. Where a new purpose or publication requires consent, the organisers obtain that consent rather than treating an update to this notice as authorisation.
